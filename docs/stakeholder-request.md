# Stakeholder Request

**Project:** Retail Sales Performance Analysis
**Prepared for:** Executive Leadership Team
**Prepared by:** Business Intelligence & Analytics Team
**Date:** July 2026

---

## Business Context

As part of the upcoming quarterly business review, the Executive Leadership Team has requested an analysis of historical retail sales data to better understand the company's financial performance across products, customers, and geographic regions.

While total sales have remained stable, leadership would like to identify the key factors that influence profitability, evaluate the effectiveness of current discount strategies, and uncover opportunities to improve business performance. The findings from this analysis will support future decisions regarding pricing, inventory planning, regional sales strategy, and customer targeting.

The analysis should use the available transactional data, including order information, customer segments, geographic regions, product categories, sales, quantity, discounts, shipping details, and profit.

---

# Business Questions

### 1. Regional & Product Performance

Which **regions**, **product categories**, and **sub-categories** generate the highest and lowest profit?

Identify areas where strong sales do not necessarily translate into strong profitability.

---

### 2. Impact of Discounts

How do different discount levels affect profitability?

* Is there a discount threshold where profits consistently decline or become negative?
* Which categories are most affected by heavy discounting?

---

### 3. Customer Segment Analysis

How does business performance differ across customer segments?

Compare **Consumer**, **Corporate**, and **Home Office** customers in terms of:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Profit Margin
* Average Order Value

Which segment provides the greatest long-term business value?

---

### 4. Sales & Profit Trends Over Time

How have sales and profit evolved over time?

Identify:

* Monthly and quarterly trends
* Seasonal peaks and declines
* Any significant changes during Quarter 4 (Q4)

Provide possible explanations based on product categories and customer purchasing behavior.

---

### 5. Product Performance

Which products and sub-categories contribute the most to business success?

Identify:

* Best-selling products
* Most profitable products
* Lowest-performing products
* Products generating high sales but low profit

Recommend which product areas should receive greater business focus.

---

### 6. Shipping Performance

Analyze shipping operations using **Order Date**, **Ship Date**, and **Ship Mode**.

Determine:

* Average shipping time by region
* Average shipping time by shipping mode
* Whether longer shipping times are associated with larger order values or lower customer profitability

---

### 7. Geographic Analysis

Compare sales and profitability across regions, states, and cities.

Identify:

* Top-performing locations
* Underperforming markets
* Geographic areas that may require strategic attention or additional investment

---

## Expected Deliverables

The analytics team is expected to provide:

* A cleaned and documented SQL database.
* SQL queries used to answer each business question.
* An interactive dashboard presenting key performance indicators (KPIs), trends, and visualizations.
* A one-page executive summary highlighting major findings, business insights, and actionable recommendations.
* Supporting documentation and screenshots of the analysis process.

---

## Project Success Criteria

The final analysis should enable leadership to:

* Improve pricing and discount strategies.
* Increase profitability across products and regions.
* Better understand customer purchasing behavior.
* Optimize shipping performance.
* Support data-driven planning for the upcoming business quarter.
